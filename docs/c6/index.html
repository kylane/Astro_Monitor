Astro Sky Conditions Monitor

Firmware updater for the Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 board

This page flashes the latest firmware straight to your device over USB — no software to install.

  1. Plug the board into your computer with a USB-C cable.
  2. Put the board into bootloader mode: hold BOOT, tap RESET, then release BOOT. (Don't hold BOOT down while plugging in the cable or before pressing RESET — only press it after the board already has power.)
  3. Click Connect and Install below, then pick the board's serial port from the browser's list.
  4. Wait for it to finish (about a minute), then press RESET once to boot the new firmware.
Your browser doesn't support this. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. This page needs to be loaded over HTTPS to flash a device — try reloading it.
This is a full reflash, so it resets the device back to its out-of-the-box state — your saved WiFi network and location settings will be cleared. After it reboots, join the AstroMonitor-Setup WiFi network again and re-enter your details, exactly like first setup.
Only works in Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS (desktop) — Safari and Firefox don't support the WebSerial connection this page uses. If the board's port doesn't show up in the list, you may need a CP210x/CH34x USB-serial driver for your OS — search for the driver name plus your operating system.
