Astro Sky Conditions Monitor

Firmware updater for the ESP8266 + 1.3" OLED build

This page flashes the latest firmware straight to your device over USB — no software to install.

  1. Plug the board into your computer with a USB cable.
  2. Click Connect and Install below, then pick the board's serial port from the browser's list — it usually shows up as CP2102 or CH340.
  3. Wait for it to finish (about a minute). The board reboots into the new firmware on its own — no buttons to press.
Your browser doesn't support this. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. This page needs to be loaded over HTTPS to flash a device — try reloading it.
Unlike the ESP32-C6 board, this update only overwrites the app itself, not the whole flash chip — your saved WiFi network and location settings should still be there when it reboots. If they aren't (or the screen shows SETUP MODE), just join AstroMonitor-Setup and re-enter them, same as first-time setup.
Only works in Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS (desktop) — Safari and Firefox don't support the WebSerial connection this page uses. If the board's port doesn't show up in the list, you may need a CP210x or CH340 USB-serial driver for your OS — search for the driver name plus your operating system.
